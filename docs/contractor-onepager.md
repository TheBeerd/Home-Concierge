## The problem with every other lead source

You already know the drill: shared leads, tire-kickers, price-shopping across five apps, and homeowners who ghost the second a cheaper quote shows up. You pay for the lead whether or not it ever becomes a job.

This isn't that.

## What this is

We're running a 90-day pilot with 20–30 HVAC contractors across DFW. Homeowners come to us with a real, urgent problem — AC out, no cooling, unusual noise — and we build a clear picture of the issue *before* it ever reaches you: symptoms, photos, system age, and what they've already tried.

You're not writing a cold quote. You're responding to a homeowner who is already informed, already comparing real options, and ready to book.

## How it works

1. **We call you, not the other way around.** When a homeowner's request matches your service area and availability, our team calls you directly.
2. **You give us your bid, we do the typing.** A 5-minute conversation covers your diagnostic fee, price range, warranty terms, and availability. We structure it — you don't fill out a form.
3. **The homeowner sees a clear comparison**, including your bid alongside others, with nothing hidden or shared with anyone outside the platform.
4. **You get the call to schedule** when the homeowner is ready to move forward — no bidding war, no auction.
5. **You pay only when a job books.** No monthly fee. No per-lead fee. No cost for a homeowner who doesn't choose you.

## What you get

- **Pre-qualified requests.** The homeowner has already described the issue, uploaded photos, and is actively deciding — not browsing five apps at once.
- **No shared leads.** Every request that reaches you is presented to a limited, disclosed set of contractors, never blasted to dozens at once.
- **A success-based cost, not a leads-based cost.** If it doesn't turn into a job, it costs you nothing.
- **A track record that follows you.** Fast response, fair pricing, and honest warranty terms build your standing on the platform over time — and get you more, and better, matches.

## What we ask of you

- An active TACLB license and current insurance, verified once at onboarding.
- A callback within the window you commit to (same day, next day — you set it).
- Straight answers on your bid: what's included, what's excluded, and what could change the price.
- Standing behind your quoted warranty terms.
